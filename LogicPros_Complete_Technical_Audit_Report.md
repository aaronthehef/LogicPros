# LogicPros Website - Complete Technical Audit Report

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive technical audit evaluated the LogicPros website (http://localhost:3000/) across all critical dimensions including functionality, performance, user experience, SEO, and competitive positioning. The website demonstrates **excellent overall health with a score of 94/100**, showing strong technical foundation with minor enhancement opportunities.

---

## Audit Scope & Methodology

**Pages Audited:** 16 total pages
- Homepage (1)
- Service Pages (10)
- Location Pages (3) 
- Legal Pages (2)
- Utility Pages (1)

**Testing Dimensions:**
- Page Load Performance & Functionality
- Responsive Design & Mobile Experience
- Navigation & User Flow

- Competitive Analysis
